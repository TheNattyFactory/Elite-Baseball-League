# EBL Terms of Use — Public Beta Draft

EBL is a beta online baseball simulation. During beta, features, rules, progression, league structures, statistics, Genesis records, and virtual items may change or reset.

Users agree to protect their login credentials, provide a usable email for verification/recovery, follow community rules, and avoid cheating, exploitation, unauthorized automation, multi-account collusion, or interference with service operation.

EBL may pause league operations, reverse clearly corrupted outcomes, suspend accounts, or take other reasonable steps to protect service and competitive integrity.

XP, awards, records, franchise access, team identities, and other virtual features have no cash value unless a future published commercial policy explicitly provides otherwise.

This draft is not a substitute for legal review. Before monetization or unrestricted public launch, EBL should obtain jurisdiction-appropriate Terms, age requirements, liability language, dispute terms, and consumer/payment disclosures.
