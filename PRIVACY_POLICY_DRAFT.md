# EBL Privacy Policy — Public Beta Draft

EBL may collect and store: username, verified email address, password hash, session/security records, IP/user-agent security metadata, player and franchise activity, game statistics, chat messages, private messages, reports, moderation actions, and operational logs.

Email is used for account verification, password recovery, security notices, and service communication. EBL should not sell personal information.

Private messages are intended for the participants, but reported messages or accounts may be reviewed by authorized moderators for safety, abuse prevention, legal compliance, and service integrity.

The production service should define data retention, account deletion, backup retention, cookie/session disclosures, regional privacy rights, and a contact method before unrestricted public launch.

This is a product draft and should receive legal review.
