# EBL Genesis — Closed Alpha RC1 Test Plan

RC1 models the alpha as a mixed league: human-owned participants enter first and CPU players occupy all remaining roster slots.

Integrity gates:
- exactly 750 roster slots across 30 franchises
- no unfilled roster slot at activation
- simulation blocked outside ACTIVE phase
- human/CPU ownership is explicit
- W/L equality and runs-for/runs-against conservation
- no negative XP wallets
- rivalry, news, record and GameCast generation remain operational
- account recovery and franchise branding tables persist
- Genesis remains resettable/archivable before hard launch

Public launch changes:
- alpha CPU fill is disabled
- position caps and human roster readiness become mandatory
- email-based password recovery replaces recovery-code-only UX
